# VR Tour sample assets

These two unchanged 8192×4096 JPEG panoramas are the source files of the optional VR Tour sample pack. They are not imported into the editor bundle or loaded at app startup. The reviewed host installer downloads the pinned public originals when the user installs VR Tour or explicitly repairs its sample pack. Documents embed the samples they use for offline preview and export.

Both assets are by Greg Zaal and are distributed by Poly Haven under CC0-1.0. See https://polyhaven.com/license and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Attribution is retained here for provenance.

- Cayley Interior: https://polyhaven.com/a/cayley_interior
- Lebombo: https://polyhaven.com/a/lebombo

They are independent sample scenes. No verified source establishes that they are rooms in the same home or spatially adjacent. Test hotspots demonstrate navigation only.

The manifest records original download URLs, exact byte lengths, SHA-256 digests, dimensions, and license evidence. No resizing, recompression, or image edits were performed.
