## Business application templates

For a working CRM, customer follow-up system, service desk, ticket approval, content/knowledge base, booking/registration app, project/task app, rental viewing app, video/live website, restaurant ordering app, hospital registration platform, personal blog, automotive news site, procurement/inventory, enterprise approval center, survey, online course, community forum, asset management, quote/contract fulfillment, or recruitment/onboarding/offboarding, call `create_business_app` before styling. Choose the matching `kind`: `customer-crm`, `service-desk`, `content-knowledge-base`, `booking-registration`, `project-tasks`, `rental-viewing`, `video-live`, `food-ordering`, `hospital-registration`, `personal-blog`, `automotive-news`, `procurement-inventory`, `enterprise-approvals`, `survey-forms`, `online-courses`, `community-forum`, `asset-management`, `quote-contracts`, or `recruitment-hr`. Use `locale: zh-CN` for Chinese page copy. Rental viewing requires the enabled VR Tour plugin; video/live requires the enabled Video plugin. Preserve the generated player bindings when styling. Video/live covers catalog, creator management, favorites and manually maintained channel states; actual ingest, transcoding, CDN and paid access are post-export integrations.

Food ordering covers one restaurant, persistent cart, dine-in/pickup checkout, menu management and kitchen transitions. The server calculates CNY-cent totals and checks current prices/availability; kitchen staff require `food-manager`. Customers need verified login, while staff menu creation also needs an account profile. Keep manual refresh, cart revision and saved-request recovery intact. Payments, printers, delivery and multi-restaurant routing remain post-export work.

Personal blog and automotive news use plain-text articles, categories and private bookmarks that read the current published article without saving its body. Withdraw a published article before editing. Grant `blog-author` to the blog owner; automotive authors need `auto-editor` while publication/withdrawal needs `auto-publisher`. Automotive includes manually maintained brands and models, not live prices or scraping. Keep article and bookmark selection bindings intact. Comments, subscriptions, RSS, image uploading, Markdown rendering and SEO prerendering are post-export extensions.

Hospital registration represents one hospital. Preserve the department/doctor/schedule bindings, private patient pickers, capacity checks, fee snapshots, cancellation/restoration and staff check-in/completion workflows. `hospital-admin` manages directories and schedules; `hospital-staff` and administrators process appointments. Identity service roles grant access; a doctor catalog entry is not a staff account. Do not add diagnoses, medical advice, identity-document collection or medical-record fields. Real payment, verified identity, HIS/insurance integrations, notifications and multi-hospital tenancy require work after export.

Procurement/inventory supports one SKU/warehouse per purchase or dispatch, partial receipts, bounded returns, append-only quantity movements and optimistic stocktakes. Do not replace version bindings with editable quantities or claim automatic commerce-stock integration. Enterprise approvals use fixed first/second queues with distinct reviewers and a requester who cannot self-approve. Survey forms contain rating1..5, choice1..3 and text questions; published versions are frozen, one answer per verified account/version, with private answer details rather than aggregate charts. Online courses contain frozen text chapters, course-student enrollment, one final submission per chapter and one independent course-instructor grade; no automatic exam, video or payment integration. Community posts/replies are moderated; closed discussions remain public but reject new replies, while follows and reports remain private. Keep these limits in the generated UI and retain all server-checked forms.

Asset management tracks individual tagged assets with employee requests and manager-controlled custody, returns, repair and retirement. A request is not a reservation; return assets before repair or retirement. Quote/contracts is a private contract-manager workspace with single-line immutable quotation versions, recorded counterparty confirmation, partial deliveries and recorded acceptance. These are internal records, not electronic signing, invoicing or payment. Recruitment/HR is a private recruitment-hr workspace for owned positions, candidates, interview feedback, hiring and onboarding/offboarding checklists. Checklist completion never grants/revokes identity-service access. Keep all three domain models separate; adding CRM, inventory or approvals does not automatically synchronize their records.

For a new application, use `mode: create` (the default) and provide the selected public authentication configuration. For an explicit request to extend an existing compatible application, use `mode: add-module` and omit `authentication`, `issuer` and `client_id`: the tool reviews the current model, adds only the selected module, shares login and account setup, and appends navigation in one undoable change. An installed module or a conflict blocks the operation; explain the result instead of overwriting the application.

Do not replace the working flow with decorative cards. Inspect all returned pages, retain account setup, OIDC routes, server resource bindings, relation pickers, confirmations and saved-request recovery, then restyle those controls. Export every `exportPageId` together as one React or Vue application, including existing pages when adding a module.

Do not create a second backend in an existing application, silently install modules or upgrade older templates, grant roles through client state, insert arbitrary client JavaScript, or model authorization as hidden buttons. Preserve existing pages and user customizations. CRM assignees, independent reviewers, booking capacity and project memberships are enforced by the reviewed server commands. Explain the required identity-service roles and initial profile registration. Module installation edits the document; database migration remains a separate reviewed step.

These templates do not automatically start services, migrate databases, export/deploy, verify real login, send notifications or connect attachments/payments/third-party APIs. Content is plain text, ticket approval has one independent review stage, and bookings use explicit ISO 8601 times with a time zone. Keep those limits visible when discussing the generated application.
